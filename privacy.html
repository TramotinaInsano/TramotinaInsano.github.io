Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Insano Poster is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own channels. It uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API) only to upload videos that the owner selects.

Data we access: the app requests only the permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) on channels the owner authorizes. It does not read, collect, or store any data about other users.

Storage and sharing: access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold or shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
